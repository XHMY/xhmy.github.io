Hi! I'm Yifan Zeng and my name in Chinese is 曾一凡. I'm a fourth-year Computer Science Ph.D. student at **Oregon State University**, advised by [Huazheng Wang](https://huazhengwang.github.io/).

My research is on **reinforcement learning for LLM agents**: training multi-turn, tool-using agents with online RL (GRPO/PPO on [verl](https://github.com/volcengine/verl)), understanding when RL helps **multi-agent LLM workflows**, and making agents and LLM rankers robust.

In summer 2026 I was a *Research Scientist Intern* at **Adobe**, where I built SkillSaw, a training-free method for optimizing agent skill libraries. Before that I was an *AI Agent Research Intern* at **AG2AI** (2025), and before my Ph.D. I worked on deep learning and computer vision in industry. I received my B.Eng. in Data Science from Zhuhai College of Jilin University in 2023, ranked first in my department.
